## 2/7 · what a witness is

Image: `brand/logo-mark.png`

```text
A witness is a key plus one fact per log: the last head it cosigned. Before cosigning a new head it checks a consistency proof that the new tree extends the old one, and persists it first. Same protocol Certificate Transparency witnesses speak: c2sp.org/tlog-witness
```

## 4/7 · erasure that keeps proofs

Image: `docs/assets/stage-5-erase.png`

```text
Leaves hold SHA-256(padded payload, 32-byte salt, index), never the content. To erase entry i, delete its salt and append a tombstone. Every inclusion proof, consistency proof and cosignature stays valid; the content is gone. Privacy requests no longer break the audit trail.
```
