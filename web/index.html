witness-log

Verify a dossier in your browser. Nothing leaves this page.

A dossier is an agent's execution record: its leaves, the checkpoint its identity key signed over them, and the receipts witnesses gave for that checkpoint. This page rebuilds the Merkle root from the leaves, checks every signature, and gives one of four verdicts. To reach WITNESSED, add a witness directory you trust: receipts are scored against your directory, never against the one inside the file.
